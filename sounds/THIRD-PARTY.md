# Third-party clips

Three of the bundled sounds are short clips of popular meme audio. They are
not original to this project and are **not covered by the MIT licence**. All
rights stay with their respective owners.

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `uthjaa.wav` | First phrase of "Uth jaa (No censor)", myinstants.com/en/instant/uth-jaa-no-censor-57562/ |
| `aayein.wav` | "Aayein Meme", myinstants.com/en/instant/aayein-meme-98363/ |
| `khatam.wav` | First word of "Rahul Gandhi khatam", myinstants.com/en/instant/rahul-gandhi-khatam-71269/ |

If you own one of these and want it removed, open an issue and it will be
taken down.

The other sounds (`chime`, `knock`, `coin`, `airhorn`, `alarm`) are
synthesised by `generate.py` and are MIT licensed with the rest of the project.
